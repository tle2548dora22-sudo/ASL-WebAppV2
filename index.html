Welcome to ASL Detector!

This app uses AI and your webcam to detect ASL hand signs in real-time — including A–Z letters and dynamic gestures.

How to get started

Click ▶ Start Camera, then place your hand inside the frame. The AI will detect and display letters automatically.

Detection modes

Choose A–Z for letters, Dynamic for gestures, or Both to use all features at once.

Save and speak

Detected text builds up in the sentence box. Click 🔊 Speak to hear it, or 💾 Save to keep it in history.
